## In plain words

Stage 1 trains the model with no extra encoder and checks that it can do the robot tasks at all. Stage 2 is the actual experiment: it trains the same model several more times, each time feeding it extra features from frozen image encoders the way the paper did (stacked as extra channels on the same grid as the video model's own latents). There are four main versions: no extra encoder (the stage 1 model), DINOv3 alone, DINOv3 paired with SigLIP 2 (a pairing the paper says helps a little), and DINOv3 paired with C-RADIOv4 (the pairing the paper says hurts). Each version is trained more than once with different random seeds, because the paper trained each version only once and so could not tell a real drop from luck. Every trained model is first tested on the normal LIBERO tasks to make sure it works, then on LIBERO-Plus, the harder perturbed benchmark where the paper saw the drop. The rule for deciding "yes, pairing DINOv3 with C-RADIOv4 really hurts on our model", "no it doesn't", or "can't tell" is written here before anything runs, so no result can move it. One honest warning, from a simulation on made-up numbers (not from data): with three seeds per version, if the real drop on our model is smaller than the paper's, "can't tell" is a likely answer. With about half the paper's drop, the rule calls it "can't tell" more often than "yes".

## Question

On the 8-layer pretrained-start world action model of stage 1 (Fast-WAM's `FastWAMJoint`, video expert loaded from Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B blocks 0-7, action expert derived from those blocks by Fast-WAM's recipe, the stage 1 recipe throughout), with frozen encoder features added by channel-wise concatenation on the VAE latent grid:

1. **Primary:** is LIBERO-Plus success with DINOv3 + C-RADIOv4 lower than with DINOv3 alone by more than training-seed noise, as the paper reports (78.59 against 84.0, a drop of 5.41 points; verified, `literature/liu2026what-table10-composition.csv` line 10 and `literature/liu2026what-table1-single-source.csv` line 4, arithmetic)?
2. **Contrast:** does the pairing the paper reports as helpful (proposed: DINOv3 + SigLIP 2, 85.83, +1.83 over DINOv3; verified, table 10 CSV line 5, arithmetic) avoid that drop on the same model?
3. **Calibration:** does DINOv3 alone beat no extra encoder on LIBERO-Plus, as in the paper (84.0 against 74.7; verified, table 1 CSV lines 3-4)? If our model does not use DINOv3 the way the paper's does, the answer to 1 is not read as a reproduction or a non-reproduction (under calibration option C-a; Open item j).

This is stage 2 of decision 006 part (c) (`decisions/006-research-question-and-done.md` line 15, verified) under the user's research question, "Why do some encoder pairings work while others make it worse, in the context of VAE-grid feature concatenation?" **The helpful pairing is the matched control.** `dinov3+siglip2` and `dinov3+c-radiov4` add the same 2,176 channels to the input (1,024 + 1,152) and the same two feature-loss terms at weight 1 (`proxy_wam.py` lines 424-433; widths from `data/README.md` lines 71-73; verified). So the contrast between them holds fixed the generic effect of "a second encoder adds channels and loss weight", which is the obvious confound in a pair-versus-DINOv3 comparison. Choosing SigLIP 2 L under Open item f (1,024 wide, recalled) would break this match (2,048 against 2,176 extra channels).

### Comparison statistic (defined before any result)
- For configuration c and training seed s: **Y(c, s)** = the mean over evaluation seeds 41, 42, 43 of that checkpoint's LIBERO-Plus success rate, each rate being total successes divided by the total number of evaluated episodes (paper App. C.2, p17, "Overall LP success is the total number of successes divided by the total number of evaluated episodes"; App. E.3, p22, "The mean is computed from the three full-benchmark success rates"), in percentage points.
- **Coverage.** Every checkpoint is meant to cover all 10,030 episodes under each evaluation seed. Episodes that raise are rerun (`rollout.py` writes them to `<out>.errors.jsonl` and resumes; docstring lines 36-38). Any still missing after reruns are left out of the denominator, as the paper's definition allows, and their count is reported per checkpoint and seed. If more than 1% of a checkpoint's 30,090 rollouts are missing (Open item j), that checkpoint counts as a missing seed.
- Across training seeds: **Ybar(c)** = mean of Y(c, s) over that configuration's n_c seeds; its spread is the sample SD over those seeds.
- Paired difference for configs A and B: **d_s(A - B) = Y(A, s) - Y(B, s)** for each seed s both have, and **dbar(A - B)** = their mean over s.
- **Training-seed noise, sigma_hat:** the pooled within-configuration SD of Y(c, s) over every configuration with at least 2 seeds: pooled variance = sum over c of sum over s of (Y(c, s) - Ybar(c))^2, divided by the sum over c of (n_c - 1). With three seeds each that is 2 degrees of freedom per configuration (8 with four configurations, 12 with six); with the extension (Decision rule, outcome I), `dinov3` and `dinov3+c-radiov4` contribute 4 each. If seeds were independent across configs, the SD of dbar over n paired seeds is sigma x sqrt(2/n): 0.816 sigma for n = 3, 0.632 sigma for n = 5 (arithmetic).
- **Evaluation-seed noise.** The binomial value sqrt(0.25 / 30,090) = 0.29 points (arithmetic) would hold only for independent rollouts, and ours are not independent: within an evaluation seed every inference call of every episode starts from the same noise (`rollout.py` docstring lines 18-20; `request_noise`, lines 112-118, reseeded with the eval seed, verified). The paper's evaluation-seed SDs exceed the binomial value (for example 1.01 for the harmful pairing, table 10 CSV line 10, against about 0.41 for a binomial at p = 0.79 over 10,030; arithmetic). So the size of evaluation noise is not known in advance. It is inside Y and sigma_hat, so the rule does not depend on it; the SD across each checkpoint's three evaluation-seed rates is reported. The same seeding gives one useful fact: for a given evaluation seed, the starting noise of the VAE channels (the first 48 channels of `randn((1, C, ...))`) and of the actions (a separate generator) is the same in every configuration, so evaluation is already paired across configurations.
- Clean-LIBERO statistic for the gate: **B(c, s)** = four-suite average on the 2,000 clean rollouts.

## Decision rule

**The threshold, the margins, the calibration condition, the clean gate and the extension are the user's decisions; none is chosen here** (Open items i, j). This section writes the rule so that it is complete and computable for every choice; the user signs off the whole rule. Once signed off it is locked and changes only by a dated amendment that states whether any stage 2 result had been seen (and, at sign-off, which stage 1 results had been seen; "Dependence on stage 1"). No verdict is read from a single training seed.

All quantities are from final checkpoints under the full protocol, with the definitions under "Comparison statistic". n is the number of paired seeds in a comparison (3, or 5 after the extension). f(n) = 2 x sqrt(2/n), two standard errors of a paired mean in units of sigma (f(3) = 1.63, f(5) = 1.26; arithmetic).

**1. Threshold T for the harm test H and the calibration test C** (options for the user; T is used only for H and C):
- **T1: T = 2.7, fixed.** Half the paper's 5.41 drop; the threshold the user locked for the proxy (proxy PLAN line 160). Does not scale with our model's level or with measured noise.
- **T2: T = 0.032 x Ybar(dinov3).** Half the paper's relative drop (5.41 / 84.0 = 6.44%, halved; arithmetic), scaled to our DINOv3 level; equals 2.7 at the paper's 84.0. It is the only option that adjusts for a low level, but at a low level it puts the H bar inside the noise: in the simulation below, with Ybar(dinov3) = 30 (T = 0.96), no true drop and sigma = 2, H comes out 12% of the time against 4% under T1.
- **T3: T = max(2.7, f(n) x sigma_hat).** The proxy's 2.7 as a floor, raised to two standard errors of the paired mean when the measured training-seed noise is larger. **Changed after review (PR #41, finding 1):** the first version also used this T for "not reproduced" and the contrast, where a larger T made those outcomes easier as the noise grew. T3 now applies to H and C only; N and K use their own margins (item 2).
- **T4: a one-sided interval.** H needs dbar <= -2.7 and dbar + t(n-1) x s_d / sqrt(n) < 0; C needs dbar >= 2.7 and dbar - t(n-1) x s_d / sqrt(n) > 0; s_d is the SD of the n paired differences and t(n-1) the one-sided 95% Student t value (2.920 for 2 degrees of freedom, 2.132 for 4; recalled table values, the implementation checks them).

**2. Margins for "not reproduced" (N) and "helpful pairing not harmful" (K)** (options; independent of the T option):
- **M1: fixed.** N if dbar(dinov3+c-radiov4 - dinov3) > -1.35; K if dbar(dinov3+siglip2 - dinov3) > -1.35. The proxy's non-inferiority margin, half of 2.7 (proxy PLAN line 157).
- **M2: equivalence form.** N if dbar(dinov3+c-radiov4 - dinov3) - f(n) x sigma_hat > -2.7; K if dbar(dinov3+siglip2 - dinov3) - f(3) x sigma_hat > -2.7. That is, a drop of 2.7 or more is ruled out at about two standard errors. More noise makes N and K harder, which pushes uncertain results into I.

**3. Calibration condition** (options): **C-a** (the proxy's form): C is required; if C fails, no reproduction or non-reproduction is claimed. **C-b**: C is computed and reported as context, and the harm outcome is read whatever C says, with a caveat when C fails.

**4. Tests** (with the chosen options):
- **C (calibration):** d_s(dinov3 - none) > 0 for every paired seed and dbar(dinov3 - none) >= T (under T4, the interval form above).
- **H (harm reproduced):** d_s(dinov3+c-radiov4 - dinov3) < 0 for every paired seed (after the extension: the sign condition chosen in item 7) and dbar(dinov3+c-radiov4 - dinov3) <= -T (under T4, the interval form).
- **N (not reproduced):** H does not hold and the chosen margin's N condition holds.
- **I (inside the noise):** neither H nor N. Precedence: H first, then N, else I. (Under T2 at a low level with M1, H and the M1 band can overlap; H wins.)
- **K (contrast):** the chosen margin's K condition. **H on the helpful pairing (H_L):** the H test applied to dinov3+siglip2 against dinov3.
- **Below both members (secondary, only if C-RADIOv4 alone is trained, Open item b):** the H test with `c-radiov4` in place of `dinov3`. Reported; it does not change the outcome, but "worse than each member alone", the paper's pattern, can be stated only when it holds.

**5. Clean gate (G), applied before each checkpoint's LIBERO-Plus evaluation** (options): **G1:** every checkpoint needs B(c, s) >= 36, stage 1's fail line, as a check that the model works, not as a result. **G2:** no gate; clean reported as context (the paper reports no clean numbers for pairs). Under G1, a checkpoint below 36 gets the stage 1 fail-branch diagnosis, which uses no test outcomes (code reading, open-loop actions on training windows, training loss against predict-zero, a unit test; stage 1 PLAN line 166), handled under item 6. If no fault is found, the low clean score is a result and the checkpoint is still scored on LIBERO-Plus, so the paired statistic has no hole.

**6. Faults, missing seeds, and keeping wave 2 whole** (closed now so that no result can steer the work):
- **Checks for every configuration before any evaluation:** the cache pre-check, the feature parity test, the first-batch report and the timing-only smoke are run for every configuration, not only for one that later surprises.
- **A fault found at any time** (by the gate's diagnosis or otherwise, always shown without test outcomes) is checked for in every configuration, fixed by PR, and every affected checkpoint in every configuration is re-evaluated, whatever its earlier result. An evaluation-side fault means re-evaluation; a training-side fault means retraining every affected run with the fixed code and the same seeds, then evaluating them. Both the old and the new results are reported, and an amendment records what had been seen.
- **Missing seeds.** A training run that fails (Design, "Training failures") is rerun once with the same seed. A checkpoint whose evaluation stays more than 1% incomplete after reruns counts as missing. If a configuration still lacks a seed, every test that needs that configuration uses only the seeds both sides have, and the H, N and C conclusions for those tests are I ("inside the noise", decided now), because the sign condition cannot be checked on all three seeds.
- **Wave 2 runs in full regardless of wave 1 outcomes.** It is not stopped, shrunk or reordered because of a wave 1 result. The only exceptions are a fault handled as above, or a stop for compute, disk or cluster reasons (Hand-off step 4), each logged with its reason.
- **Scope cut if evaluation is too expensive** (pre-registered; the cost line is Open item l). If the measured evaluation cost of the whole design exceeds the agreed line, cut in this order and no other: (1) LIBERO-Plus for `siglip2` and `c-radiov4` alone (if trained; the "below both members" test is then not reported); (2) the helpful pairing entirely (K and H_L not tested; any H result is reported without the selectivity claim). Never cut: three seeds of `none`, `dinov3` and `dinov3+c-radiov4`, and the full protocol on every evaluated checkpoint (no subset). The cut is decided from the measured cost before wave 2's evaluations start, not from any outcome.

**7. The extension, if the result is I** (pre-registered now; whether it is allowed is Open item i): once, train seeds 3 and 4 of `dinov3` and `dinov3+c-radiov4` (4 runs, about 96 L40S-hours by the sizing basis, at least, plus 4 evaluations). Then: n = 5 for H and N; sigma_hat pooled over all configurations with n_c - 1 degrees of freedom (2 for `none` and the helpful pairing, 4 for the two extended configurations, 2 for each single encoder if trained); f(5) = 1.26 in T3 and M2; t(4) = 2.132 in T4; the sign condition is one of **S-all** (all five d_s < 0) or **S-4of5** (at least four of five negative), the user's choice. C and K stay on seeds 0 to 2. The result is reported as a two-stage design. If still I, it is reported as inside the noise and the user decides.

- **C fails, under C-a.** The model does not use DINOv3 as the paper's does. The pairing comparisons are reported, but no reproduction or non-reproduction is claimed ("don't interpret", proxy PLAN line 158). Clean LIBERO and LIBERO-Plus rollouts are the test sets.
