    1. The pretrained-start model with no extra encoder, clean training and clean testing on LIBERO, clears a pass bar written before it runs.

Pairings fused the paper's way (channel-wise concatenation on the VAE latent grid): DINOv3 alone, one pairing the paper reports as helpful and the one it reports as harmful, on clean LIBERO first and then on LIBERO-Plus, where the paper's drop was measured, before any claim about the reported harm.
