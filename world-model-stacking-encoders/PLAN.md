2. **Fusion-step suspects test (conditional; user, 2026-11-06).** Runs only if the proxy replication (stage 4) finishes, calibration passes, and D+R vs D lands in the "proxy does not show a substantial effect" branch of the locked outcome rule. If the proxy does show the drop, the locked rule applies unchanged; if calibration fails, no fusion tests. The idea: the way channels from different encoders are combined may be why D+R hurts. Three suspects: (1) scale, one source dominating the concatenated vector (the paper says only "source-specific processing"; our proxy z-scores each channel, our choice); (2) double counting, C-RADIOv4 partly distilling DINOv3 (stated by the paper, Sec. 3.2 and 4.4; not tested by us) while each source's future-feature loss has weight 1.0; (3) grid mismatch, our proxy resizes C-RADIOv4's grid to the VAE grid, 14 x 14 per camera (our choice; the paper says features are aligned to the VAE latent grid, Sec. 3.3, but not how). The test would change our own choices where the paper is silent, one at a time, and see whether the D+R drop appears. Our choices may be hiding the drop we are trying to reproduce (speculative). The design is written later and goes to the user for sign-off before anything runs. Open questions and the evidence for each suspect: `writing/hypotheses-draft.md` "Update 2026-11-06: fusion-step suspects"; decision record: LOG 2026-11-06 [agent, planning].
