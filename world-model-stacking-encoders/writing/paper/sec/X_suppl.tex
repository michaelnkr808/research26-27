\subsection*{Clean-frame tests: detail}
\label{sec:suppl-clean}

% Config: Tally lines 6, 17-23; Target lines 17-18.
\paragraph{Setup.}
% Tally line 6: 63,728 time steps, split 38,464 / 12,416 / 12,848 frames, 100 test demos.
The 63,728 frames split by demonstration into 38,464 training, 12,416 validation and 12,848 test frames, with 100 test demonstrations.
% Tally line 23: alpha tuned on validation separately for every probe and pair, grid 1e-2..1e5, refit on train+val.
The ridge strength was tuned on validation separately for every probe and pair (grid $10^{-2}$ to $10^{5}$) and the probe refit on training plus validation data.
% Tally line 23: alpha hit the bottom edge (0.01) for SigLIP 2 global, C-RADIOv4 mean, VGGT-Omega global and mean.
% Tally line 19: CKA on 5,000 frames, seed 0. Tally lines 14-15: SAM 3 has no summary token so its global features are its patch mean; VGGT-Omega's are its camera token.
CKA uses a fixed subsample of 5,000 frames (seed 0).
SAM~3's global features are the mean over its patch tokens, and VGGT-Omega's are its camera token.
% Tally line 23 (continued):
The ridge strength reached the bottom edge of the grid (0.01) for the SigLIP~2 and VGGT-Omega probes on global features, for C-RADIOv4 on mean features and for VGGT-Omega on mean features.

% fp32 vs fp16: Tally line 107 and lines 11-15 (dtype column); Setup, "Rules fixed in advance".
\paragraph{Deviation from the pre-registration.}
DINOv3's features are 32-bit floating point and the partners' 16-bit (C-RADIOv4 and VGGT-Omega under autocast), because DINOv3 in 16-bit gave non-finite features on the first batch.
% Tally line 107, LOG.md 2026-10-19 (e) (line 194): the pre-registration fixed 16-bit features.
This deviates from the pre-registration, which fixed 16-bit features.
The target-side test reads the same caches.

% No matched-dimension control: Tally line 17 ("pre-registered"), line 103; Target lines 18, 57 (dimension confound).
\paragraph{No matched-dimension control.}
The concatenated global probe inputs have 4,096 dimensions for DINOv3 with SigLIP~2 or SAM~3, 6,144 with VGGT-Omega and 6,656 with C-RADIOv4.
Ridge strength is tuned per probe, but nothing equalizes capacity, and no matched-dimension control was run in this test.
On the target side the two widest partners, VGGT-Omega (4,096 dimensions for both cameras) and C-RADIOv4 (4,608), are first and second under the primary measure; this confound was not tested further.

% Renderer caveat: README.md line 29 (perturbed-frames PLAN Amendment 2026-11-09, around line 314); the "orderings
% within the tally are unaffected" inference is the lead's and not verified there (README.md line 29).
\paragraph{Renderer.}
Absolute probe numbers are specific to the stored frames: the same DINOv3 probe lost about 0.05 $R^2$ between the stored frames and our re-render of the same moments.
% Source: experiments/2026-11-06-perturbed-frames/PLAN.md, Amendment 2026-11-09 (internal reference, kept out of the printed text).
We therefore compare orderings, not absolute values.
All tally probes used the stored frames.

% Input-side design without a rule: Tally line 3; Tally lines 43-45, 110-115 (skipped encoders).
\paragraph{Input side: no decision rule.}
The input-side test fixed its choices (encoders, feature types, the global features as primary and mean features as secondary, the bootstrap) before running, but states no hypothesis and no rule; it is a sanity check on four pairs, not a statistical test.
% Tally line 3 and line 102 (n = 4 pairs, no significance reported).
Three of the paper's seven DINOv3 partners (V-JEPA~2.1, UniTok, HRP) were not run.

\paragraph{Target side.}
The primary reading is the reduced-rank ridge interference with rank 64, the per-block scaling $w=1/d$ (a$'$), global features and the mean over the three horizons.
% Input side: Tally line 22 and src/diagnostics.py (review finding 9: probes kept fixed, test demos resampled). Target: Target lines 23, 56, 60.
In both tests the intervals are demonstration-level bootstrap intervals (1,000 paired draws, seed 0) with the fitted models held fixed, so they exclude fitting variance.
The target-side test is a linear, reduced-rank stand-in for the world model's flow-matching objective on clean frames; it tests whether co-target interference shows up in the features, not what happened inside the paper's model.
The reference $R^2$ for DINOv3's change alone is 0.364 to 0.372 across pairs.
The outcome rule and a secondary prediction (that C-RADIOv4's interference shrinks when block variances are equalized) were both fixed in advance; the secondary prediction also fails, since C-RADIOv4's interference grows from $+0.0151$ under a$'$ to $+0.0251$ under equalized variance (b).
Table~\ref{tab:suppl-target} gives the other readings.
% T0: Target line 22 ("B's share of the initial loss"); "predict-the-mean": src/target_diagnostics.py line 190 at 52d1226;
% table values Target lines 33-36.
