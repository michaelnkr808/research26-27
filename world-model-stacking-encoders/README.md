## Key findings so far
Signed off by the user 2026-10-21 (input side) and 2026-10-27 (target side). Sources: `experiments/2026-10-19-feasibility-tally/` and `experiments/2026-10-21-target-diagnostics/` (one suite, 4 pairs, frozen features, linear probes). Paper deltas: liu2026what Tables 1 and 10 (`literature/liu2026what-table{1,10}-*.csv`).
- **The simplest form of the idea fails the cheap check** (verified, n = 4 pairs). The paper ranks the DINOv3 (D) pairs L (+1.83) > S (+0.49) > G (+0.40) > R (−5.41). Neither frozen-feature measure singles out D+R, the pair the paper reports as harmful:
  - **CKA:** R is the most similar to D in all 6 feature-type × camera cells. But L, the best of our four partners by the paper's numbers, is nearly as similar (global agentview 0.576 vs 0.583), and G, the least similar by far (0.21 / 0.27), gave only a small gain.
  - **gain(B|D)** (global): R adds the most action-relevant information on top of D: +0.037 [95% CI +0.032, +0.042], vs S +0.033, L +0.017, G +0.014. That is the opposite of the paper's ranking for R. R's interval overlaps S's (tally README, line 86). No CIs were computed for CKA (tally README, line 79).
- **On frozen features, every partner helps a little** (verified). All 4 gains are positive and small (+0.014 to +0.037 probe R²), and all partners probe about as well as D alone (R² 0.61–0.67; proprio 0.28). So the reported D+R drop does not show up in a linear readout of frozen features, and the mismatch is not explained by a broken encoder: the sanity checks match the pilot exactly.
- **The target-side version fails too** (verified, n = 4, pre-registered rule LOG 2026-10-21 (i)). In the WAM each encoder is also a future-feature target, so we measured how much adding B's future as a co-target hurts a rank-64 linear prediction of D's future. G interferes most (+0.034 [+0.033, +0.035] R²), then R (+0.015), L (+0.014), S (+0.003). R is not the largest, so H-E fails at this level; R's interference also grows, not shrinks, when encoders' losses are equalized. The ordering doesn't track the paper (L and R nearly tie here but are the paper's best and worst).
- **Not settled** (speculative): why D+R hurts. Neither the input nor the target role shows it on clean frozen features. Possible reasons:
  - noise from the paper's single training checkpoint per combo (its eval-seed sd for D+R is 1.01, the largest in Table 10, but training-seed noise is unmeasured);
  - training dynamics that frozen features can't show;
  - the perturbed LIBERO-Plus test conditions;
  - our measures are the wrong ones.

  The scales also differ: paper Δ is success-rate points, ours is probe R². Only orderings are compared.
  The two answer keys differ in kind, too. Ours is per frame: the action the human teleoperator recorded at that frame of a clean LIBERO-Goal demo (or D's real future features), with no robot ever acting. The paper's is per episode: whether the trained robot, acting on its own in a perturbed LIBERO-Plus scene, completed the task (success or fail). Our probes measure whether frozen features describe clean demos; the paper's reported drop appears in closed-loop behaviour under perturbation.
- **Absolute probe R² values in the tally are specific to the stored renderer** (verified, `experiments/2026-11-06-perturbed-frames/PLAN.md` Amendment 2026-11-09, around line 314): the same DINOv3 probe lost about 0.05 R² between the stored frames and our re-render of the same moments, so the tally's numbers are not directly comparable with re-render numbers. Orderings within the tally are unaffected (the lead's inference, not verified: all tally probes used stored frames).
- **Correction 2026-11-09:** the earlier wording "L, the paper's best pair" was wrong: D+U (UniTok) is higher (+2.12 vs +1.83; `literature/liu2026what-table10-composition.csv`; direction `LOG.md` 2026-11-07 [agent, slides], line ~1315). L is the best of the four partners we tested.
- **Cost** (verified): large encoders are slow to extract (SAM 3 at 1008 px: 5.5 frames/s on a 2080 Ti; ~10 GPU-h for D, S, G on one suite). Diagnostics are cheap once features exist: 30–50 CPU-min per pair.
- Starting point: liu2026what (arXiv 2610.13024v1) judges complementarity qualitatively and uses one training checkpoint per config. See `literature/notes/liu2026-what-visual-representations-wams.md`.
