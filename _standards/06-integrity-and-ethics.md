# 06 — Integrity and Ethics

- **Report what happened.** Negative and null results get logged and are worth reporting, not hiding.
- **No selective reporting.** If you ran 10 configurations, say so, even if you only present the best one.
- **Don't move the goalposts.** If the metric or hypothesis changes after seeing results, record that change explicitly.
- **Attribute properly.** Credit code, data, ideas, and help: collaborators, advisors, open-source authors, and AI tools as required.
- **Respect licenses and terms** for every dataset, model, and piece of code you use.
- **Protect people's data.** Anything involving human subjects or personal data needs IRB/ethics review where required, minimal collection, and secure storage. Never commit it to a public repo.
- **Consider misuse and impact** for work that could be dual-use, and note it in the direction's `README.md`.
- **Fix errors openly.** When a mistake is found in reported work, correct it and log the correction, including what changed.
