- **Choose the base model.** The lead leans to WNM-3D (2026-11-18, "it makes more sense to do WNM-3D since its a navigation model"); no decision record yet, goes to the PI on 2026-11-19. After the simulator finding below was put to the lead again: "WNM-3D seems fine" (2026-11-18). Open check answered 2026-11-18: WNM-3D's released training and evaluation run in its own simulator (GN-Bench on Gaussian-splat scenes), not in Habitat, where SoundSpaces 2.0 renders audio. Using it needs Habitat-rendered audio-visual data converted to its LeRobot training format and a new evaluation link to Habitat (`LOG.md` 2026-11-18 addendum). Simulator gap looked into by the literature session (PR #68, part 2): no existing Habitat benchmark is a drop-in; WNM-3D's Habitat (VLN-CE R2R) variant and its action converter are not released; UniWAM (Wan2.2-5B, trained and evaluated in Habitat, code and gated checkpoints released) is the closest complete stack found, with a different task and a different habitat-sim version from SoundSpaces 2.0's. The planning lead suggested the lead keep WNM-3D for the PI meeting and name UniWAM as the alternative; the lead has not answered. PI, 2026-11-19: "if you can use WNM-3D's own simulator for simulating audio-visual training data, then we will not use SoundSpaces 2.0". Check answered 2026-11-19 (PR #71, part A): GN-Bench has no audio as released (code read and searched), but adding it looks possible with extra work: run an acoustic engine (RLR Audio Propagation, the engine inside SoundSpaces 2.0, or pyroomacoustics as a lower-fidelity fallback) on the InteriorGS floor plans and object boxes, with materials assigned from labels. A precedent for ray-tracing sound through a mesh built from a Gaussian-splat reconstruction exists (pi-AVAS, ICCV 2025); nobody was found doing it on InteriorGS or a navigation simulator. So two routes are open: stay in GN-Bench and build the audio, or move to Habitat with SoundSpaces 2.0 and build the WNM-3D link. Neither has been sized; the choice is the lead's and the PI's. Candidates: `literature/survey/2026-11-16-summary.md`.
