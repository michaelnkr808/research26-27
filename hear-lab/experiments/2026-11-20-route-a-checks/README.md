## Results

**Check 1, sound engine and geometry.** Not passed by the PLAN's rule (8 PASS, 4 FAIL). What the run shows (`verified`):
- RLR-Audio-Propagation at 4fd446b loads on Ubuntu 22.04 (glibc 2.35) through the ctypes binding and returns one-channel and two-ear impulse responses for a real InteriorGS scene. First arrival matches the straight-line distance within 0.33 ms. One impulse response takes 0.02 to 0.23 s on one CPU thread depending on the settings.
- The left-ear-first test fails in both settings sets. The diagnostic shows why: the library's default head model, as configured, gives a strong, correctly signed level difference between the ears but almost no time difference (within plus or minus 0.09 ms at every angle in free field). So the two-ear audio from these settings carries a level cue and essentially no timing cue. Follow-up (2026-11-20, rental 3): with a measured HRTF file (MIT KEMAR, normal pinna) loaded through `RLRA_SetListenerHRTF`, a time difference between the ears appears, close to a human-sized head's (0.73 ms at 90 degrees in free field, left ear first for a source on the left; one dummy head) and the check 1 left-ear test passes in both settings sets (left ear leads by 0.68 ms). The check 1 result with the default head model stays a FAIL. Which head model route A uses is for planning to decide. A higher spherical-harmonic order was not tried.
- Floor plans line up with GN-Bench's occupancy maps by eye in all four scenes (no flip, offset or scale error; looked at by the experiment lead agent, not yet by the user). The pre-set wall-share bar (0.9, occupied = value 0) passed in 2 of 4 scenes (0.928, 0.927) and failed in 2 (0.870, 0.857); under GN-Bench's "not passable" reading all four are 0.927 or more. The misses sit on grey unknown border cells and in openings.
- Every goal `label_ins_id` was found in `labels.json` (four scenes).

**Check 3, visible starts.** Passed by the PLAN's rule, provisionally: the by-eye review of the 20 images was done by the experiment lead agent and waits for the user. With the proposed depth rule (tolerance 0.15 m): test_unseen 1114 of 5000 starts have the target visible, 3886 not; the train_seen sample 351 of 1000 visible, 649 not. The split barely moves with the tolerance (0.05 m and 0.30 m in `LOG.md`). No goal id was missing. The GN-Matrix goal position sits a median 1.26 m (test_unseen) from the target object's box (horizontal distance to the box, zero inside it), with a quarter of episodes more than 3.6 m away. Limits: in 39 of 939 scenes a mirrored frame fits the object boxes better than the one used, so the flags of those 233 test_unseen and 41 train_seen episodes may be wrong; no test_unseen path is shorter than 3.01 m, so the path-based within-3 m flag is empty there.

**Check 2, timed training step.** Ran on Vast.ai on 2026-11-21 (instance 61731856, 1x H200, 3.8048 USD/h, 2.15 h, 8.20 USD by the hourly rate; account credit down 8.29 USD). Detail and deviations in `LOG.md` (2026-11-21). With the released DeepSpeed config (ZeRO-2), the full fine-tune does not fit on one H200 at any batch size from 12 down to 1: every trial runs out of memory when the optimizer state is allocated at the first step. With the PLAN's fallback (optimizer state offloaded to CPU, a deviation from the released config) batch 12 fits: median 34.6 s per step over 50 timed steps (0.35 samples/s), peak GPU memory 65 GiB allocated, 76 GiB reserved. The GPU averaged about a third busy; the released launcher caps CPU threads at one, which most likely makes the CPU-side optimizer step the bottleneck (not profiled). 46 of the 48 rendered episodes were used (the released converter drops 2 as too short; the user approved). Larger offload batches and more CPU threads were not tried.

## What these results do not show

Nothing here is a result about audio helping navigation. The visibility rule is a proposal for this count; the rule for the paper is set later. Check 2 times training only on one GPU; it says nothing about how a model trained this way navigates. Whether the missing timing cue matters for the research question has not been tested.
