## Answers in one place

- **Q1 (audio or another new input into a pretrained video diffusion model, Wan first): found, partly.** Adapter-only training on a frozen Wan backbone is published and reported to work for audio (MultiTalk, Wan2.1-14B; Face-to-Face, Wan2.1-1.3B). The only numeric frozen-against-LoRA-against-full audio comparison found (OmniAvatar, Wan2.1-14B) has the frozen version clearly weaker on lip sync than LoRA plus new modules. The nearest match to WNM-3D's backbone (TalkVerse, Wan2.2-5B) uses LoRA plus fully trained audio cross-attention, on about 6.3k hours of data and a large GPU cluster, so it is not a cheap recipe. **Not found:** a frozen-against-LoRA ablation on Wan2.2-5B; an audio adapter on Wan with a reported cost near this budget; anything with spatial or multichannel audio as the input to a Wan model.
- **Q2 (world action models or video-based robot policies that added an input after pretraining with the backbone frozen or LoRA-only): not found in the form asked.** No paper found trains only an added input module on a frozen Wan-based world action model and compares it with full fine-tuning. Audio in robot policies exists only in vision-language-action models (frozen backbone plus trained audio modules in ListenVLA; LoRA in Audio-VLA) and in ManiWAV (trained end to end), none with a full-fine-tuning comparison. What exists on frozen against full: DreamZero says LoRA gave "suboptimal results" (no numbers, 14B); one LoRA-against-full number on Wan2.2-TI2V-5B (RynnWorld-Teleop, generation not policy): full is slightly better; AstraNav-World trains Wan2.2-5B with LoRA and says it was more stable (no comparison shown).
- **Q3 (Spatial-AST): found.** Input format, output shape and training data are verified from the paper and repo code (below). One published use at a short window (1 s) was found, with the whole encoder fine-tuned. No streaming or sliding-window use found. Whether NaVLA2 uses a 1 s window is not stated in its main text.

## What WNM-3D itself does (verified from the saved HTML of arXiv 2608.07267 v3)

Only the VGGT-Omega geometry encoder is frozen. The 3D Scene-to-Token Adapter (23.97M trainable parameters, Table 5) and the Wan2.2-TI2V-5B world-action backbone are trained together ("VGGT-Omega remains frozen while the Adapter and world-action backbone are optimized", Sec 4.1). Training used 16 H100 GPUs, global batch 192, learning rate 1e-5 (Table 6, App. A.3). GPU-hours and step counts are not given in the text read. No freeze-against-fine-tune ablation and no LoRA appear. So WNM-3D is evidence that a small adapter plus a trained backbone works, not that an adapter alone does. The cost of that route (16 H100) is why it does not fit the budget.

## Q3: Spatial-AST

Spatial-AST is the spatial audio encoder from BAT (Zheng et al., ICML 2024, arXiv 2402.01591; repo zszheng147/Spatial-AST, CC-BY 4.0, based on Audio-MAE).

- **Input** (`verified`, paper Sec 4.1/5.1 and the repo's `spatial_ast.py`): 10 s, two-channel binaural, 32 kHz; STFT window 1024, hop 320, 128 mel bins; log-mel of left and right plus the cosine and sine of the interaural phase difference weighted by the mel filter bank, stacked to 4 x 1024 x 128. Ambisonics are future work in the paper and "soon" in the README.
- **Output** (`verified`, code): 16 x 16 patches give 512 patch tokens plus 3 class tokens (category, distance, direction) = 515 tokens of width 768, from a 12-layer encoder initialised from Audio-MAE. The released `forward()` returns only the three head outputs; the tokens need a wrapper (NaVLA2's code has one with a test comment of shape [batch, 515, 768]). Parameter count is not stated (a 12-layer width-768 transformer is roughly 86M, `speculative`).
- **Short input** (`verified` in code, effect `speculative`): if the input has fewer than 1024 frames, the spectrogram is interpolated along time up to 1024 frames; a 1 s clip is stretched, not padded. The position embedding is a fixed table for 512 patches. So short clips run without code changes but are out of distribution relative to 10 s training.
- **Training data** (`verified`, Sec 3.1, Table 2): SpatialSoundQA, AudioSet clips (355 event labels; 20K split 18,373 clips, 2M split 1,861,750) spatialised with SoundSpaces 2.0 reverberations on Matterport3D (21,131 reverberations over 90 buildings). Objective: cross-entropy on category, distance (0.5 m bins) and direction (1 degree bins). The released checkpoint is `SpatialAST/finetuned.pth` in the Hugging Face dataset repo zhisheng01/SpatialAudio; which AudioSet split produced it is not stated (the repo script is the 20K recipe: 4 GPUs, batch 64, 50 epochs). The Hugging Face model page returned 401, so no model card was read. Reported sample test numbers in the README: 67.62 distance accuracy and 18.00 degree angular error on 17,148 test items.

**Who feeds it what** (window length per use):
- **Hear You Are** (Ryu, Chung, Harwath, CVPR 2026; full PDF read locally): frozen Spatial-AST ("kept frozen throughout the entire training process"), Q-Former projector with 64 audio query tokens, 10 s binaural audio at 32 kHz, SigLIP2 vision with LoRA, Qwen2-7B with LoRA rank 16, 3 epochs on 8 A5000 for about three days, batch 128 (`verified`). This corrects the worker's "unread, freeze status unresolved".
- **HLR-LLM** (arXiv 2610.21588; full HTML read locally): frozen Spatial-AST encoder, trainable MLP projector, LoRA rank 16 on Qwen2.5-Omni, curriculum in three stages (projector only first), 2 epochs on four A6000 with per-device batch 4; BAT preprocessing at 32 kHz. Clip length and tokens per clip not stated (`verified` absent in the text read).
- **NaVLA2** (AAAI-26; local PDF, main text): CLAP on the mono downmix and Spatial-AST on the binaural signal, two-layer MLP adapters, audio updated every time step, stage 1 trains the audio adapters on 813K spatial-audio-text pairs for 2 epochs, LoRA rank 16 on Vicuna-7B. Per-step window length, token count to the LLM, and encoder freeze status: not stated in the main text read. The code wrapper's 32000-sample test input is 1 s only if the rate is 32 kHz (`speculative`).
- **Learning Spatially Aware Audio-Visual Features from Videos with Mono Audio**, arXiv 2611.19593: the only found use of Spatial-AST on short clips: 1 s binaural clips, the whole encoder fine-tuned, tokens mean-pooled (read as a fetch extraction; authors not captured).
- **SAVi** (Chen et al., CVPR 2021; local PDF): a 1 s binaural clip per step as two 65 x 26 spectrograms, a different, small encoder, not Spatial-AST (`verified`).
- **SAIL** (arXiv 2609.34347) and **Spatio-Temporal Audio Language Modeling** (arXiv 2606.14141): frozen encoder plus trained Q-Former or projector with LoRA on 10 s clips; the second adapts Spatial-AST to first-order ambisonics and uses short crops for one baseline (length not stated). OmniEcho (arXiv 2609.23407) uses its own encoder, spatial audio from the current step's window, frozen in its last stage.

**Not found:** streaming or sliding-window use of Spatial-AST; a per-step window with the encoder frozen; a released model card; the exact AudioSet split of the released checkpoint.

## What this means for the lead's budget idea (`speculative`, for the planning lead to weigh)

1. "Frozen audio encoder plus trained projector" is the standard recipe for audio into language models (HLR-LLM, Hear You Are, SAIL), and it ran on four to eight consumer-class GPUs for days. It is not evidence for video diffusion backbones.
2. For video diffusion, published practice is a zero-initialised cross-attention or gated branch, trained with the backbone frozen or LoRA-adapted; the audio-driven talking-video papers show it works but at 70 to over 6,000 hours of data. The one numeric ablation (OmniAvatar) says LoRA on the backbone beats a fully frozen backbone at following the audio.
3. The Spatial-AST path for per-step windows is unproven: published use is 10 s clips, and the one 1 s use fine-tunes the whole encoder. Per-step windows would be a departure from every verified recipe; the stretch-to-1024-frames behaviour for short input would need a test, not an assumption.
4. This file does not say what will fit 200 dollars; sizing is the experiment session's.
