- **Options considered:**
  1. Does direction matter (question 1). Chosen.
  2. Question 1 plus an encoder comparison: does a spatial audio encoder that ranks better on a spatial audio benchmark also navigate better. Not found in prior work. Each extra encoder is another training run, and it needs a benchmark to rank the encoders by; RMS-AQA was that benchmark and is parked. Deferred.
  3. Does the model need to predict future sound, or is hearing it enough. Partly done in other settings, and Wan 2.2 has no audio output path. Set aside by the lead on 2026-11-18.
  4. The PI's example, long-form audio processing, as the question now. The literature session found no audio vision-language-action model that takes long-form or streaming long audio (`literature/survey/2026-11-19-pi-followups-simulator-audio-papers-gaps.md`, part C). What the PI means by "long-form" for this work is not confirmed. Deferred by the lead ("we can focus on long form tasks later").
- **Decision & rationale:**
  - **(a) The question.** When the target is out of sight, does a navigation world action model reach a sounding object more often with spatial (two-ear) audio than with one-channel audio or with no audio? The test is three versions of one model (decision `002`): no audio, one-channel audio, spatial audio. The task is walking to an object that is making sound. Success is reported split by whether the target was visible at the start. This sits inside the direction's broad question, which stays as worded by the lead.
  - **(b) What is claimed as new, and what is not** (from PR #68, parts 3 and 4; the "still new" list is the literature session's reading, `speculative`). Already known, so not claimed: audio beats no audio in audio-goal navigation; and NaVLA2 has one spatial against one-channel table, which is not controlled (its two branches use different encoders, and no seeds or run count are stated). Not found in prior work: a controlled comparison of the three audio conditions inside a pretrained video world action model; a split of navigation success by whether the target is visible at the start; and whether audio improves the predicted frames for regions out of view. The expected direction of the answer (spatial beats one-channel) would not be surprising by itself, so the contribution has to be the controlled comparison and the visibility split.
  - **(c) Deferred, not dropped:** the encoder comparison (option 2) and the long-form or memory angle (option 4). Each would be an extension of the same three-version setup. Predicting future sound (option 3) stays set aside.
  - **(d) RMS-AQA stays parked.** The lead parked the PI's first task on 2026-11-18 ("maybe just drop this for now"), and the PI's written feedback of 2026-11-19 did not bring it back. It returns to `PLAN.md` "Now" if the PI asks for it or if option 2 is taken up.
  - Rationale (the planning lead's, accepted by the lead's "go"): question 1 is the smallest version of the lead's idea that the searches did not find done, and options 2 and 4 both build on it.
  - Nothing here is a result. No number was measured for this record.
