# 003 — Simulator route A: WNM-3D's own simulator plus a sound engine, with a measured HRTF
- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Status:** proposed 2026-11-22 on the lead's "commit to route A it sounds good" in the planning window; accepted when the lead gives the merge OK for this pull request. The PI has not been told of the commitment and has not answered whether this route meets the PI's condition (see Consequences).
- **Context:** WNM-3D's simulator (GN-Bench on InteriorGS scenes) has no audio, so the audio-visual training data and the closed-loop test have to come from somewhere (decision `002`, Consequences). Two routes were sized without running anything (`writing/2026-11-19-sizing-comparison-simulator-routes.md` and the two route memos, PR #73). What followed:
  - The PI, in writing after the 2026-11-19 meeting: "if you can use WNM-3D's own simulator for simulating audio-visual training data, then we will not use SoundSpaces 2.0" (`LOG.md` 2026-11-19, PI feedback).
  - The lead, 2026-11-19, on the planning lead's recommendation to start with route A and commit only after real checks: "yeah thats fine" (`LOG.md` 2026-11-19).
  - The experiment session ran three checks on Vast.ai on 2026-11-20 and 2026-11-21 (`experiments/2026-11-20-route-a-checks/`, PRs #75 to #80; figures below are from that folder's `README.md` and `LOG.md`, `verified` there, not rerun for this record):
    - Check 1, the sound engine. RLR-Audio-Propagation at `4fd446b` loads on Ubuntu 22.04 and returns one-channel and two-ear impulse responses for a real InteriorGS scene; one response takes 0.02 to 0.23 s on one CPU thread. The check did not pass by its own pre-set rule (8 PASS, 4 FAIL): the left-ear-first test failed in both settings sets, and the wall-share bar (0.9) passed in 2 of 4 scenes (0.928 and 0.927 against 0.870 and 0.857). With the library's default head model the two ears differ in level but hardly in time (within 0.09 ms at every angle in free field). In a follow-up with a measured HRTF file (MIT KEMAR, normal pinna) loaded through `RLRA_SetListenerHRTF`, the time difference is 0.73 ms at 90 degrees in free field and the left-ear test passes in both settings sets. The floor plans line up with the occupancy maps by eye in all four scenes; that look was the experiment lead agent's, not yet the lead's.
    - Check 3, visible starts. With the proposed depth rule, 1114 of 5000 test_unseen starts have the target in view and 3886 do not; in a 1000-episode train_seen sample, 351 and 649. Limits: in 39 of 939 scenes a mirrored frame fits the object boxes better (233 test_unseen and 41 train_seen episodes); the GN-Matrix goal position is a median 1.26 m from the target's box in test_unseen, with a quarter of episodes more than 3.6 m away.
    - Check 2, one timed training step. The released full fine-tune does not fit on one H200 at any batch size from 12 to 1; with optimizer state offloaded to CPU, batch 12 runs at a median 34.6 s per step. This is the same in both routes (sizing comparison, "What is the same in both routes").
  - The lead, 2026-11-22, asked "1. remind of routeA". The planning lead described both routes and said what committing means: "we stop keeping route B warm and build rung 2 on WNM-3D's own simulator, using the measured head recording. Route B stays written down as the fallback if something breaks." The lead: "commit to route A it sounds good".
- **Options considered:**
  1. **Route A.** Keep WNM-3D's renderer, scenes, episodes, converter, trainer and closed-loop client as released, and add an acoustic engine (RLR-Audio-Propagation) that computes what the agent hears at each pose, from geometry built out of the InteriorGS floor plans and object boxes with assumed materials. Chosen.
  2. **Route B.** Move WNM-3D to Habitat and use SoundSpaces 2.0 on scanned scenes. The audio tool is the familiar one and other audio navigation papers use its episode sets, but the released weights have never seen those scenes, the scene sets need access requests, and the connection between WNM-3D and Habitat has to be built (sizing comparison). Kept as the written fallback; no work on it is planned.
  3. **Route A through an audio-enabled Habitat-GS or habitat-sim build** instead of the bare RLR library. This was the fallback if the bare library did not load (sizing comparison, recommendation item 3). It loaded, so this is not needed.
- **Decision & rationale:**
  - **(a) Route.** Route A. Reasons: it is the route the PI named, on the condition that it can make the data; the released model already runs in these scenes, so no training budget goes to new rooms; and the two route-specific unknowns the checks covered came back usable (the engine runs on a rented Linux machine, and both the visible and the not-visible group are large). The training cost found in check 2 does not separate the routes.
  - **(b) Head model.** Two-ear audio is rendered with a measured HRTF, not the library's default head model. The starting file is the one the follow-up used (MIT KEMAR, normal pinna, `mit_kemar_normal_pinna.sofa`, sha256 in the experiment `LOG.md`); the experiment `PLAN.md` for the audio rendering names the file it uses. Reason: decision `001` asks about spatial audio, and audio with a level cue but almost no timing cue is a weaker form of it (the planning lead's judgement, `speculative`; whether the missing timing cue changes navigation was not tested).
  - **(c) What this record does not decide.** The audio encoder, the audio window per step, the one-channel definition, the instruction text, the visibility rule for the paper, the success measure, the transmission and ray settings, the materials mapping, and how the model is trained. These belong to later decision records or to experiment `PLAN.md` files (`PLAN.md`, the ladder).
  - Nothing here is a result about audio helping navigation.
- **Consequences:**
  - `README.md` "Open scope questions": where the audio-visual data comes from is marked decided. `PLAN.md`: the route item moves out of "Now" and "Next"; rung 2 of the ladder (render and check the audio on a small set) is built on this route. `STANDARDS.md` Scope points to this record.
  - **For the PI, still open:** route A keeps the PI's scenes, pictures and episodes, but the audio physics comes from RLR, the engine inside SoundSpaces 2.0. Whether that counts as "not using SoundSpaces 2.0" is the PI's to say (sizing comparison). If the PI says no, this record is reopened.
  - **A difference from SoundSpaces-based work, to state in any write-up:** SoundSpaces' continuous simulator sets no HRTF, so it most likely renders with the default head model (experiment `LOG.md` 2026-11-20, `speculative` until a SoundSpaces 2.0 render is measured). Audio from this route would then differ from that work in its timing cue. Spatial-AST, the leading encoder candidate, was trained on audio spatialized with SoundSpaces 2.0 (`literature/survey/2026-11-21-new-input-training-recipes.md`), so how it reads audio with a measured HRTF is unknown and belongs to the encoder decision.
  - **The sound is approximate.** Walls come from floor plans, furniture is boxes, and materials are assumed from object labels (route A memo). Results are stated as "with assumed materials". The task is self-made, so no other audio navigation paper has results on it.
  - **Carried into rung 2 and the comparison's experiment `PLAN.md`:** resolve or exclude the 39 scenes whose object boxes may be mirrored before any count is reported; decide where the sound source sits and how success is measured, given the distance between the goal position and the object's box; state the transmission setting, since it changes what a hidden target sounds like; the wall-share bar missed in 2 of 4 scenes (the misses are on unknown border cells and in openings); the lead's own look at the overlay and visibility images.
  - **Terms.** RLR-Audio-Propagation is archived, ships Linux x64 binaries only and is CC-BY-NC. The InteriorGS terms have not been read, so whether rendered audio may be released is unknown, and raw outputs stay out of git until they are read. For the KEMAR file no formal licence was found; MIT's page says the measurements are "made available to the research community" (experiment `LOG.md` 2026-11-20).
  - **Route B** stays the fallback if route A fails at a later rung. Its first check would be the SoundSpaces install on a rented machine (sizing comparison).
